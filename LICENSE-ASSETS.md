# Asset Licensing

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers this repository's **source code only**. It
does **not** automatically cover files under `assets/` (sprite sheets, icons,
audio, etc.).

- The default `assets/species/placeholder/` sprites shipped in this repo are
  original, programmer-generated placeholder art (solid-color blobs), released
  under CC0-1.0 — free to use, modify, and redistribute with no attribution
  required.
- Any other species/content added to `assets/species/<id>/` must record its
  own license and authorship in that species' `species.json` under
  `attribution`. Do not assume MIT or CC0 applies unless a given asset says so.
- Contributors adding new art must own the rights to it or use properly
  licensed/CC0 work. Do not submit existing copyrighted character art,
  including from other video games, anime, or franchises. See
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
